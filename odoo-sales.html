The problem

Challenges in resort & hotel operations

These are the exact pains we heard in the field — and what they cost you every week.

📅

Inefficient booking

Enquiries scattered across email, Facebook, WhatsApp, Viber & Booking.com — rates aligned by hand, overbooking risk, slow confirmations.

🧾

Complex billing

Room + dives + bar reconciled manually at checkout; VAT & agent commissions poorly recorded; non-compliant receipts.

👁

Limited data visibility

No live occupancy, RevPAR, dive utilisation or cash position — decisions made on gut feel.

🧹

Housekeeping & comms gaps

Dive & kitchen run on a whiteboard and group chats with no accountability — guests fall through the cracks.

🤿

Unrecorded services

Paper order slips and gear rentals get lost — “if the paper is lost, the revenue is not realized.”

💸

Cash leakage

No real inventory or spend control. The #1 owner concern: money quietly leaking from over-spending and untracked sales.
