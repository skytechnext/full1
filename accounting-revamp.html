01Why an overhaul — not a tidy-up

Casa Escondida runs five businesses under one roof, but the money is recorded as if it were one shoebox. The result is slow closes, manual re-keying, and the very thing the owners flagged in discovery: repeat errors. The fix isn't a new bookkeeper — it's a single automated ledger where each transaction is born once, in the system that created it, and flows to the books untouched.

02Target architecture — one ledger, fed by everything

Every operational app already posts to Accounting in Odoo. The overhaul wires them all to a single PH chart of accounts, tags each line with its department, and lets tax + bank automation do the rest.

🧾 Core ledger

Odoo Accounting + l10n_ph (PH chart of accounts & tax). Double-entry, full audit trail, period locks, multi-currency (PHP base, USD for OTA/foreign).

🔌 Feeder apps

Hotel (folios), POS (Restaurant + Dive Shop), Sales/eCommerce, Purchase, Inventory (COGS), Payroll, Fixed Assets — each posts journal entries automatically on its own events.

🏷 Analytic layer

Analytic plan = Profit centre: Rooms · Dive · Restaurant · Retail · Courses. Every income/expense line is tagged, giving live per-department P&L without a second set of books.

No hand-keyed re-entry anywhere in this chain — humans approve and review, they don't transcribe.

03Automation principles

① Capture at source

A transaction is recorded once, by the app that created it (POS sale, room checkout, vendor bill scan). Accounting receives a posting, never a re-typed copy.

② Touchless by default

Routine postings, tax calc, depreciation, recurring entries and bank matches run automatically. Humans act only on exceptions and approvals.

③ Rules > memory

Reconciliation models, fiscal positions and tax tags encode the "right answer" so it can't be forgotten — directly attacking the repeat-errors goal.

④ Controls baked in

Approval tiers, segregation of duties, period locks and a full audit log are configuration, not discipline.

⑤ Compliance by construction

BIR VAT, withholding and invoice formats are computed on every line, so returns are a report — not a month-end scramble.

⑥ One source of truth

Owners, heads and the accountant read the same live dashboards. No reconciling versions of the truth.

04Chart of accounts & books of accounts

The foundation everything posts to. We adopt the l10n_ph PH chart, extend it for a resort + dive operation, and structure analytic tags so a single posting answers both "what kind of account?" and "which business?".

Chart of accounts (PH-aligned)

  • Assets — Cash on hand, BPI/BDO bank, GCash, AR (guests/OTA/agents), inventory (F&B, retail, dive gear, Nitrox/O₂), input VAT, creditable withholding (2307s received), prepaid, fixed assets & accumulated depreciation.
  • Liabilities — AP (vendors), output VAT, VAT payable, withholding tax payable (EWT/compensation), SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG payable, guest deposits / advances (deferred), gift-card/voucher liability, accrued expenses.
  • Equity — owners' capital, retained earnings, drawings.
  • Income — Room revenue, Dive (fun dives, courses, gear rental), F&B, Bar, Retail, Transport/park-fee pass-through, other.
  • COGS / Expenses — F&B cost, retail cost, dive consumables, salaries & benefits, OTA commissions, utilities (incl. Starlink), repairs, depreciation, marketing.

Books of accounts (computerised)

  • General Journal, General Ledger.
  • Cash Receipts & Cash Disbursements books.
  • Sales & Purchases books.
  • All generated automatically from journal entries — registered with BIR as a Computerised Accounting System (CAS) / Books, with the required Acknowledgement Certificate or Permit-to-Use for sales machines.

Odoo: account.account · account.journal · analytic plan "Profit centre" · fiscal positions

Known blocker (from the build log). The active chart of accounts cannot be swapped to the l10n_ph template while posted journal entries exist — it needs an empty-books cutover window (see §15 Migration). 12% VAT is already in use in the interim.

07Bank, GCash & cash — reconciliation that runs itself

The single biggest day-to-day time sink in most resorts. Odoo turns reconciliation from a monthly marathon into a near-daily, mostly-automatic review.

How it works

  • Statement in: bank feed (where supported) or one-click import of BPI/BDO/GCash statements (CSV/OFX).
  • Reconciliation models: rules that auto-match a statement line to an invoice/bill, or auto-create the entry (e.g. bank charges, interest, GCash fees) — the bulk match without a human.
  • Suggestions: fuzzy-match on amount/reference/partner for the rest; one click to confirm.
  • Exceptions only: staff look at the handful that don't match — that's where errors hide.

Cash discipline

  • POS drawer counts at every session close create the cash journal entry.
  • Daily cash deposit recorded; bank credit auto-matches the deposit.
  • Petty cash as its own journal with receipts attached.
  • Variances surface immediately, not at month-end.

Target: ~90% of bank lines auto-reconciled · reconciliation done within a day, not a month

Automated: statement import · model-based matching · auto-entries for fees/charges · payment matching. Human gate: confirm suggested matches + clear exceptions.

09Payroll & statutory

~36 staff across dive, kitchen, housekeeping, front office and admin — much of it seasonal. Payroll feeds the ledger directly and carries the statutory deductions every PH employer owes.

The run

  • Attendance / roster → payslips per pay period.
  • Statutory deductions computed: SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG (HDMF), withholding on compensation.
  • 13th-month accrual tracked through the year.
  • One payroll journal posts gross, deductions-payable and net to the ledger.

Automated outputs

  • Employee payslips + BIR 2316 at year-end.
  • Remittance schedules for SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG.
  • Compensation withholding flows to 1601-C / 1604-C.
  • Labour cost lands in each department's P&L via analytic tags.
Automated: deduction calc · payroll journal posting · statutory + 2316 outputs · analytic labour costing. Human gate: approve the payroll run before posting/paying.

10Fixed assets, deferrals & revenue recognition

Fixed assets & depreciation

  • Boats, compressors, dive gear, vehicles, building improvements, Starlink/IT capitalised with useful lives.
  • Odoo posts depreciation automatically each period — no manual schedules.
  • Disposals/sales handle gain/loss and stop depreciation.
  • Gear linked to Maintenance so a unit in service is unavailable for rental.

Revenue recognition

  • Guest deposits / advances → deferred-revenue liability, released to income on stay.
  • Prepaid dive packages & courses → recognised as dives/sessions are delivered.
  • Gift cards / vouchers → liability until redeemed.
  • Keeps income in the right period — cleaner P&L and audit.
Automated: recurring depreciation entries · deferred-revenue release schedules · voucher liability tracking. Human gate: set useful lives / recognition schedule at setup.

11Live P&L per department (analytic accounting)

Analytic accounting answers it continuously: every income and cost line carries a profit-centre tag, so a real P&L for Rooms, Dive, Restaurant, Retail and Courses exists at any moment — no second ledger, no month-end allocation exercise.

Tag once

POS, folios, bills and payroll auto-apply the profit-centre tag from the product/department — staff don't choose it.

Report anytime

Per-department revenue, COGS, labour and contribution margin; drill from a dashboard tile to the underlying entries.

Decide faster

Feeds the report's RevPAR/GOPPAR/capture KPIs and the Profit Estimator with real numbers, not guesses.

14Controls, security & audit

Segregation of duties

Who can create a bill ≠ who approves payment ≠ who posts. Role-based access per department head vs. accountant vs. owner.

Approval tiers

POs and payment batches route by amount; large or unusual items need a second approver before money moves.

Period locks

Once a month is closed and filed, the period is locked — no silent back-dated edits.

Full audit trail

Every posting is logged with who/when; reversals are explicit entries, never deletions.

Attachments on entries

The scanned bill, OR, or contract is stapled to its journal entry — audit-ready on click.

Bank-feed integrity

Reconciliation against the actual statement catches leakage and fraud early, not at year-end.

15Migration & cutover plan

The overhaul is sequenced around one hard constraint: the PH chart of accounts can only be adopted cleanly on an empty-books window. We pick a cutover date, freeze the old books, load opening balances, and run parallel until trusted.

StepWhat happens
1 · DesignFinalise PH chart of accounts, journals, tax tags, fiscal positions, analytic plan, approval tiers.
2 · AccreditationRegister Odoo as CAS / get POS machines BIR-accredited; approve the invoice series. (Gating for go-live selling.)
3 · Cutover dateChoose a clean fiscal boundary (ideally year/quarter start) so the l10n_ph CoA can be adopted with no posted entries blocking it.
4 · Opening balancesImport trial-balance opening figures, open AR/AP, inventory valuation, asset register with accumulated depreciation, deposit liabilities.
5 · Master dataCustomers, vendors (with TINs for 2307), products with tax tags, employees with statutory IDs.
6 · Parallel runRun Odoo alongside the old method for one cycle; reconcile totals; train heads + accountant.
7 · Go-liveSwitch off the old books; Odoo becomes the single source of truth; hypercare support.
The l10n_ph swap is the long pole. Posted journal entries block the CoA template change, so the cutover date must land where books can be empty (fresh fiscal period or a fresh database seeded with opening balances). Plan filings around it.
