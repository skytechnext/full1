Odoo Demo Walkthrough Guide

A 20-minute click-through of the live demo — what to show, what to say, and the pain each step removes.

⏱ Flow: open with the guest-facing website, follow one booking all the way to the books, then show the dive & back-office depth, and finish on automation + dashboards. Tie each screen back to a pain they told us about.
Step 0

Login & orientation

  1. Go to edu-escondida.odoo.com/odoo and sign in.
  2. Show the Apps grid — “one system, not eight.” Point out PHP currency & the Casa Escondida branding/logo.
Say: “Everything you run — rooms, restaurant, dive shop, stock, money — lives here. No more spreadsheets and group chats.”
Step 1

Guest website & online booking

  1. Open /shop, /book, /dive-center, /restaurant, /events.
  2. Show 42 published products with photos & prices; add a room + a dive to the cart.
Pain removed: enquiries scattered across FB/WhatsApp/Viber with manual rate alignment.
Say: “Guests book themselves, in their language, 24/7 — and it lands straight in your system.”
Step 2

Booking → Sales → folio

  1. Open Sales → show the ~45 orders; open one with room + dives + meals.
  2. Explain the single folio and the 50% deposit flow.
Pain removed: room + dive + bar reconciled by hand at checkout; overbooking risk.
Say: “One booking, one folio — every charge follows the guest automatically.”
Step 3

Restaurant POS — floor plan & kitchen

  1. Open Point of Sale → Casa Escondida — Restaurant & Room Service → Resume/New Session.
  2. Show the 4 floors (Open-Air Dining, Beachfront Deck, Bar Lounge, Pool Deck) and the table layout.
  3. Ring up a few items; show Kitchen Display (Preparation) and the charge-to-room payment.
  4. Open the second config Dive Shop & Retail.
Pain removed: paper chits lost = unrecorded revenue; charges on the wrong guest.
Say: “If it’s punched in, it’s billed and counted. The kitchen sees orders instantly.”
Step 4

Inventory & cost control

  1. Open Inventory → show on-hand stock on F&B/bar/retail; a POS sale decrements it live.
  2. Mention the 20-pack minimum & auto-reorder.
Pain removed: the owner’s #1 worry — cash leaking from untracked stock & spend.
Say: “Sell 5 of 10 sodas, 5 remain — and the books update at the same time.”
Step 5

Dive operations

  1. Calendar/Appointments for trips; Project for the 4 course cohorts; Maintenance for 14 gear/compressor/boat equipment + service requests.
  2. Show HR employees & instructor skills.
Pain removed: whiteboard + group-chat hand-offs where a guest once went un-attended.
Say: “Boats, guides, gear and certs — all scheduled, confirmable and logged.”
Step 6

CRM pipeline

  1. Open CRM → 30 opportunities across sources (Website/FB/WhatsApp/Agent/Booking.com); show persona tags.
Say: “Every enquiry becomes a tracked opportunity — and feeds automated marketing.”
Step 7

Accounting & reports

  1. Open Accounting → 18 customer invoices + 10 vendor bills posted with 12% VAT.
  2. Show Reporting → Profit & Loss and the VAT report.
Pain removed: books “a big mess” on VAT/commissions; non-compliant POS receipts.
Say: “Live P&L and tax-ready records — no month-end scramble.”
Step 8

Automation

  1. Settings → Technical → Scheduled Actions → show the 7 “CE ·” automations (dynamic pricing, dive briefing, overbooking guard, review request, reorder, cert reminders, win-back).
  2. Settings → Technical → Automation Rules → the booking-confirmation welcome message.
Say: “The system works overnight — pricing, reminders, briefings — so your team doesn’t have to.”
Step 9

Close the loop

  • Recap the 6 pains → each now solved on screen.
  • Show the phased rollout & packages.
  • Next step: confirm Phase 1 (POS + Inventory), add Webkul PMS + QloApps channel manager, and the l10n_ph books window.
Say: “This isn’t a slideshow — it’s your resort, already running on Odoo. Phase 1 can go live in weeks.”
