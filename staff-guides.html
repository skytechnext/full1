Getting started (everyone)

First-day basics for the shared POS/office devices.
  1. Open the browser and go to edu-escondida.odoo.com (or the office bookmark).
  2. Log in with your department account (shared logins per department — not personal). Ask your manager for the password.
  3. Top-left grid ⋮⋮⋮ switches between apps (Reservations, POS, Inventory, etc.). You only see the apps for your role.
  4. Always log the action in Odoo instead of paper or group chat — if it's not in Odoo, it didn't happen and the money isn't counted.
The whole point of the new system: one place for bookings, dives, food, stock and money — so nothing gets lost on paper or in chats.

Reservations & Front Desk

For Ms. Eloisa's team. Replaces manual rate-aligning and scattered messages.

Take a booking

  1. App: Reservations / Sales → New.
  2. Search or create the guest (name, email, phone, country, DOB). Tag the persona (local / overseas / agent / event) and channel (Website / Booking.com / Walk-in / Agent).
  3. Add the room for the date range — Odoo shows only rooms that are free, so you can't double-book.
  4. Add dive package, meals (full board +₱1,500/pax), transfers (van) and any extras as lines.
  5. The price comes from the Rate Manager; apply guest discount if eligible (repeat ≤10%, agent 20%, instructor 10%).
  6. Send the quote → take the 50% deposit → status becomes Confirmed and the room is blocked.

Check-in / Check-out

  1. Check-in: open the booking, capture ID/passport (upload), confirm requests (diet, birthday, transfer).
  2. During stay, all charges (dives, bar, food) flow onto the guest's one folio automatically.
  3. Check-out: open folio → review → choose group or individual billing → generate the VAT official receipt → take payment (cash/card/GCash/bank) → mark room for housekeeping.
Cancellations: rebook allowed if ≥7 days before; no-show forfeits the 50% deposit; medical/emergency = rebook with no forfeiture. Record the reason in the booking.
Booking.com bookings appear automatically (via the channel manager) — no more manual copying. Just confirm and proceed.

Dive Center

For Dong's team. Replaces the whiteboard + group chat.

Daily brief & manifest

  1. Each morning, read the AI Daily Brief in the group (weather, tides, boats, guides, gear, park fee). React ✅ to confirm your assignment.
  2. App: Dive / Calendar → today's trips show boat, guide and guests. Open a trip to see the digital manifest.
  3. Before the dive: confirm tanks/Nitrox, gear and that each guest has a signed waiver/medical (e-sign) and recorded certification level.

During & after the dive

  1. Record gear rentals and extra tanks on the trip (tap the items) — this bills the guest's folio automatically.
  2. After the dive, mark it complete and log notes / guest dive log.
  3. If gear is damaged: open Maintenance → New Request on that item — it's automatically blocked from rental until serviced.
Dives must be arranged ≥2 days ahead so boats are available. If a guest isn't on the manifest, they don't dive — confirm with reservations first.

Kitchen, Bar & POS

Phase 1 priority. Replaces paper chits — every sale is captured.

Take an order (Restaurant)

  1. App: Point of Sale → open your session (start cash float).
  2. Pick the table on the floor plan → add menu items → send to kitchen (kitchen ticket prints/shows).
  3. Payment: choose Charge to Room (posts to the guest folio) or Cash / Card / GCash.
  4. For walk-ins / Booking.com guests without a folio, settle immediately at the POS.

Bar & retail

  1. Use the Bar zone for drinks; stock decreases automatically as you sell.
  2. Dive-shop retail uses the retail POS; low-stock items trigger a reorder.
No more lost paper slips = no more lost revenue. If it's punched into POS, it's billed and counted.
Always close your POS session at end of shift so sales post to accounting and the cash count matches.

Housekeeping

  1. App: Housekeeping / Tasks → see rooms needing cleaning (auto-created at checkout).
  2. Update status: Dirty → Cleaning → Ready. Front desk sees readiness live — speeds up early check-ins.
  3. Report any maintenance issue from the room task (photo + note) → goes to maintenance automatically.
  4. Record linen/amenity usage so stock stays accurate.

Inventory / Stores

Phase 1 priority (POS ↔ Inventory ↔ reports).
  1. App: Inventory. Every POS/kitchen/bar/dive sale reduces stock automatically (e.g. 10 soft drinks → sell 5 → 5 left).
  2. Keep the 20-pack minimum rule: items below minimum auto-create a purchase request (RFQ).
  3. Receive deliveries against the purchase order so cost and stock update together (FIFO).
  4. Do periodic counts via Physical Inventory; investigate any variance (leakage control).
  5. Dive-side consumables: O2, chlorine, tanks, compressor diesel — track here too.

Accounts

For the accounts club + corporate accountant. Fixes the VAT/commission/expense issues.
  1. Sales, POS and bookings auto-create VAT (12%) invoices / official receipts — no manual typing.
  2. Reconcile bank, card, GCash and Booking.com payouts (arrive 8–9 days later) against invoices.
  3. Record vendor bills against purchase orders; pay suppliers; track AP.
  4. Agents are net-priced (e.g. retail $100 → agent pays $80) — record the net rate received; no commission payable to track.
  5. Petty cash: enter expenses (Lenny/Ellen) → Dong approves in Odoo.
  6. Month-end: run P&L, balance sheet, VAT report and trial balance from Accounting → Reporting.
The POS must be BIR-registered and issue compliant official receipts (the old one lapsed). Confirm accreditation before go-live.

Manager / Owner (Andrew, Dong, Eloisa)

  1. Dashboards: occupancy, RevPAR, dive utilisation, F&B sales, cash position — live, no waiting for reports.
  2. Rate Manager: set base rates and toggle weekend/season/occupancy/sea-view/LOS modifiers; one save updates website, Booking.com and POS.
  3. Approvals: discounts, refunds, petty cash and purchases route to you for one-tap approval.
  4. Cash-leakage watch: variance alerts (stock counts, voids, comps) land in your inbox — Andrew's #1 priority.
  5. Marketing: review CRM segments and automated campaigns (reviews, win-back, overseas/Chinese-language).
