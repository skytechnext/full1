🛎️ Front Desk & Reservations

For Ms. Eloisa's team. Replaces manual rate-aligning and scattered messages across email / FB / WhatsApp / Viber / IG.

📈 The booking lifecycle

1💬EnquiryWebsite, Booking.com, walk-in, agent
2👤Guest recordTag persona + channel
3🛏️Add room + extrasOnly free rooms show
4🏷️Rate Manager priceApply eligible discount
5📤Send quoteTake 50% deposit
6🔒ConfirmedRoom blocked, folio opens
  1. App: Reservations / Sales → New.
  2. Search or create the guest (name, email, phone, country, DOB). Tag the persona (local / overseas / agent / event) and channel (Website / Booking.com / Walk-in / Agent).
  3. Add the room for the date range — Odoo shows only rooms that are free, so you can't double-book. Note only 8 rooms are sea-view.
  4. Add dive package, meals (full board +₱1,500/pax), transfers (van) and any extras as lines.
  5. Price comes from the Rate Manager; apply guest discount if eligible: repeat ≤10%, agent 20% (net), instructor 10%.
  6. Send the quote → take the 50% deposit → status becomes Confirmed and the room is blocked.
Quote & accept now in PHP (was USD). The price you see is the price on the website, Booking.com and POS — all from one Rate Manager.
  1. Open the booking → capture ID / passport (upload — stored for the DOT guest-arrival report).
  2. Confirm requests: diet, birthday, transfer, dive plan.
  3. Hand over — during the stay, all charges (dives, bar, food, transfers) flow onto the guest's one folio automatically.
Overseas guests fill weekdays; locals fill weekends. Most guests are repeat & direct — greet them by name from their profile.
  1. Open the folio → review every line.
  2. Choose group or individual billing (no service charge).
  3. Generate the VAT official receipt (12%).
  4. Take payment: cash / card / GCash / bank transfer.
  5. Mark the room for housekeeping → a cleaning task is created automatically.
BIR: the POS must issue compliant official receipts. Confirm BIR accreditation before go-live (the old one lapsed).
  • Rebook allowed if ≥7 days before arrival.
  • No-show forfeits the 50% deposit.
  • Medical / emergency = rebook, no forfeiture.
  • Always record the reason in the booking.
Booking.com bookings appear automatically via the channel manager — no more manual copying. Just confirm and proceed. (Booking.com collects full payment and remits in 8–9 days.)

🤿 Dive Center

For Dong's team. Replaces the whiteboard + group chat — the #1 pain that once left a guest un-attended.

🌊 Dive day flow

1🤖AI Daily BriefWeather, tides, boats, guides, gear, park fee
2✅ConfirmReact ✅ to your assignment
3📋Digital manifestBoat, guide, guests per trip
4🧪Pre-dive checksTanks/Nitrox, gear, waiver & cert
5🐠DiveLog gear rentals / extra tanks
6📝Log & billMark complete → folio charged
  1. Each morning, read the AI Daily Brief in the group (weather, tides, boats, guides, gear, park fee). React ✅ to confirm your assignment.
  2. App: Dive / Calendar → today's trips show boat, guide and guests. Open a trip for the digital manifest.
  3. Before the dive: confirm tanks / Nitrox, gear, and that each guest has a signed waiver / medical (e-sign) and recorded certification level.
Dives must be arranged ≥2 days ahead so boats are available. If a guest isn't on the manifest, they don't dive — confirm with reservations first.
  1. Record gear rentals and extra tanks on the trip (tap the items) — this bills the guest's folio automatically.
  2. After the dive, mark it complete and log notes / the guest's dive log.
  3. Dive consumables (O₂, tanks, compressor diesel) draw down from Inventory automatically.
Beginners / Discover Scuba and certified fun-divers can be tagged on the trip so guides know the mix before they hit the water.
  1. Damaged gear? Open Maintenance → New Request on that item.
  2. The item is automatically blocked from rental until serviced — no more handing out broken kit.
  3. Track service history per item; cert-renewal reminders fire automatically.

🍽️ Kitchen, Bar & POS

Phase 1 priority. Replaces paper chits — every sale is captured. Two POS configs: Restaurant & Room Service and Dive Shop & Retail.

🧾 The order-to-cash flow

1💵Open sessionStart the cash float
2🪑Pick tableFloor plan: 4 floors, 23 tables
3🍳Send to kitchenTicket prints / shows
4💳PaymentCharge to Room / Cash / Card / GCash
5🔒Close sessionCash counted, posts to accounting
  1. App: Point of Sale → open your session (start cash float).
  2. Pick the table on the floor plan → add menu items → send to kitchen.
  3. Payment: choose Charge to Room (posts to the guest folio) or Cash / Card / GCash.
  4. Walk-ins / Booking.com guests without a folio → settle immediately at the POS.
Always ask room/name before "Charge to Room" — charging the wrong folio is the #1 POS mistake.
  1. Use the Bar zone for drinks; stock decreases automatically as you sell (10 in → sell 5 → 5 left).
  2. Dive-shop retail uses the Dive Shop & Retail POS; low-stock items trigger a reorder.
  3. Nitrox & consumables are storable too — they draw down as sold.
No more lost paper slips = no more lost revenue. If it's punched into POS, it's billed and counted.
  1. At shift start: open session & declare the opening float.
  2. At shift end: close session → count cash → Odoo flags any difference.
  3. Closing posts all sales to accounting and updates stock.
Never leave a session open. If you don't close it, sales don't post and the cash count won't match — that's exactly the leakage Andrew is watching.

🧹 Housekeeping

Live room status the front desk can see — speeds up early check-ins, no more verbal hand-offs.

🚪 Room status — how it moves

Check-out posted 🔴 Dirty 🟡 Cleaning 🟢 Ready Available in Rental
  1. App: Housekeeping / Tasks → see rooms needing cleaning (auto-created at checkout).
  2. Update status as you work: Dirty → Cleaning → Ready. Front desk sees readiness live.
  3. Report any maintenance issue from the room task (photo + note) → goes to Maintenance automatically.
  4. Record linen / amenity usage so stock stays accurate.
A "Ready" room shows green to the front desk instantly — that's how an early-arriving guest gets checked in faster.

📦 Inventory / Stores

Phase 1 priority (POS ↔ Inventory ↔ reports). Keep the 20-pack minimum; receive on FIFO; counts catch leakage.

🔁 The stock loop

1🛒SalePOS / kitchen / bar / dive reduces stock
2⚠️Below 20-pack min?Auto-creates a purchase request (RFQ)
3📄Purchase orderManager approves & sends
4📥Receive (FIFO)Cost & stock update together
stock is topped up — and the loop repeats with the next sale
  1. App: Inventory. Every POS / kitchen / bar / dive sale reduces stock automatically.
  2. Items below the 20-pack minimum auto-create a purchase request (RFQ).
  3. Receive deliveries against the purchase order so cost & stock update together (FIFO).
  4. Do periodic counts via Physical Inventory; investigate any variance (leakage control).
  5. Dive-side consumables — O₂, chlorine, tanks, compressor diesel — track here too.
Variance is watched. A count that doesn't match expected stock raises an alert to the manager — keep counts honest and investigate gaps the same day.

  • ✓Action an auto-generated RFQ / purchase
  • ✓Receive a delivery against a PO (FIFO)
  • ✓Run a physical count & investigate variance

💰 Accounts

For the accounts club + corporate accountant. Fixes the VAT / commission / expense mess.

💸 Money flow

1🛒Sales & POS & bookingsAll transactions
2🧾Auto VAT (12%)Invoices / official receipts
3🏦ReconcileBank, card, GCash, Booking.com payout
4📊ReportsP&L, VAT, balance sheet, trial balance
  1. Sales, POS and bookings auto-create VAT (12%) invoices / official receipts — no manual typing.
  2. Reconcile bank, card, GCash and Booking.com payouts (arrive 8–9 days later) against invoices.
  3. Record vendor bills against purchase orders; pay suppliers; track AP.
  4. Agents are net-priced (retail $100 → agent pays $80) — record the net rate; no commission to track.
  5. Petty cash: staff enter expenses → Dong approves in Odoo.
  6. Month-end: run P&L, balance sheet, VAT report & trial balance from Accounting → Reporting.
BIR: the POS must be BIR-registered and issue compliant official receipts (the old one lapsed). Confirm accreditation before go-live.
Currency is now PHP everywhere (SGD/USD retired). 12% VAT is applied in the meantime while the PH chart of accounts swap awaits an empty-books window.
