North star

The thesis: own “Anilao, perfected”

Don't compete on price with Crystal Blue or Buceo. Win by being the warmest, most photographer-obsessed, most sustainable resort in the macro capital of the world — then monetise that love through experiences, content and direct relationships. Three game-changers carry most of the upside:

💎

1 · Yield, not just occupancy

Variable pricing + direct booking + ancillary upsells lift RevPAR 12–22% and recover 15–18% OTA leakage — with zero new rooms.

📸

2 · Experiences & content

Photo/video, signature blackwater nights, events & courses turn a bed-night into a high-margin, shareable experience that markets itself.

🌏

3 · Fill the weekdays

Chinese-language funnel + dive-club & corporate partnerships fill the weekday/off-peak troughs locals don't — pure incremental margin.

Prioritisation

Where to start — quick wins vs. big bets

⚡ Quick wins (now, low effort)

  • Dynamic pricing + direct-booking shift
  • Photo/video upsell + referral loop
  • Signature blackwater & bonfire nights
  • Automated reviews + AI concierge
  • Beginner/family experiences

🎯 Big bets (higher effort, transformative)

  • Chinese-market funnel + channel manager
  • Events/weddings as a product line
  • Photographer residencies & IDC academy
  • Green Fins + conservation positioning
  • Brand build / second property

🌗 Fill the troughs (incremental margin)

  • Weekday corporate & club buy-outs
  • Workation / digital-nomad packages
  • Off-peak promos to overseas markets

🚫 Be careful with

  • Price cuts (erodes the premium-trust position)
  • Over-reliance on OTAs (commission + data loss)
  • Owning a liveaboard before proving demand (partner first)

The payoff
Stacked, these moves target a +30–45% revenue uplift over three years, materially higher margin (less leakage, less OTA commission), a stronger brand moat, and an estimated ₱30M+ of enterprise-value uplift from more durable, better-documented cash flows — turning a great little resort into the definitive Anilao experience.
