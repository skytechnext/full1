01Purpose & document scope

This document baselines what Casa Escondida needs from the new ERP and why — the agreed reference against which the build, UAT and acceptance are measured. It consolidates several artefacts that are usually separate: the BRD (business needs), FRD (functional behaviour), NFRs (quality attributes), data/integration requirements, acceptance criteria and a traceability matrix.

In scope

  • One ERP across Hotel/PMS, Restaurant & Bar POS, Dive Center, Retail, Inventory, Accounting, CRM/Marketing, Website/eCommerce, HR & Maintenance.
  • The AI/automation layer (concierge, dynamic pricing, dive briefing, guards).
  • BIR-aligned finance & the accounting overhaul.
  • Data migration, training, go-live & hypercare.

Out of scope (this programme)

  • Physical network / Starlink hardware procurement (assumed in place).
  • Construction/renovation of the property.
  • Third-party OTA contracts themselves (Odoo integrates; commercial terms are the owner's).
  • Statutory tax filing itself (Odoo prepares; the accountant files).

02Business context & drivers

Casa Escondida Anilao is an owner-led PADI 5-Star dive resort (24 rooms, 8 ocean-view) running five businesses under one roof — rooms, dive center, restaurant/bar, retail and courses — today coordinated by paper, spreadsheets and group chats. Discovery surfaced consistent, costly friction.

DriverWhat's happening todayDesired outcome
Cash leakage (owner's #1 concern)No live inventory/spend control; "if the paper is lost, the revenue is not realised"Every sale & cost captured at source; live stock & cash position
Repeat errors (owner's success metric)Manual re-keying across POS, folios, booksCapture once, rules carry it through — errors designed out
Scattered enquiriesFB / WhatsApp / Viber / Booking.com handled by hand, slowlyOmni-channel capture + AI concierge → CRM
OTA dependencyCommission erodes margin; phasing off Booking.comDirect bookings via own site; OTA as supplement

08Data & integration requirements

Data migration

  • Customers/guests, vendors (with TINs for 2307), products & rates, employees (statutory IDs), gear/serials.
  • Opening balances: trial balance, open AR/AP, inventory valuation, fixed-asset register, deposit liabilities.
  • Cleansing & de-duplication; reconcile control totals before sign-off.

Integrations

  • OTAs (Booking.com) — channel sync / commission capture (phasing down).
  • Payments — card & GCash; bank statement feeds/imports.
  • Email/SMS/messaging for guest comms & the AI concierge.
  • BIR EIS e-invoicing — ready if/when mandated.
Key constraint: the l10n_ph chart-of-accounts adoption needs an empty-books cutover window (posted entries block the template swap) — see the migration plan on the accounting page.

09Assumptions, constraints & dependencies

Assumptions

  • Reliable internet (incl. Starlink) at the property.
  • Owner/heads available for discovery, UAT & training.
  • Accountant available for BIR/CAS specifics.
  • Hardware (POS terminals, printers) available or procured.

Constraints

  • BIR accreditation lead time gates go-live selling.
  • CoA swap needs an empty-books window (timing).
  • Seasonal peak — avoid go-live in the busiest window.
  • Small team — change capacity is limited; phase it.

Dependencies

  • BIR RDO approvals (CAS/POS, invoice series).
  • Bank feed availability / statement formats.
  • OTA/channel-manager subscription for two-way sync.
  • Source data quality from existing records.

10Acceptance criteria & UAT

A requirement is "done" when its acceptance test passes in UAT, witnessed by the responsible stakeholder. Representative criteria:

ReqAcceptance test
FR-PMS-02A guest with room + a dive + a bar tab checks out → one VAT-correct invoice; revenue tagged Rooms/Dive/F&B.
FR-POS-03A POS sale of a stocked item decrements stock and posts COGS on session close.
FR-ACC-01Quarterly VAT (2550Q) figures reconcile to the ledger with no manual adjustment.
FR-ACC-02An imported bank statement auto-reconciles ≥ 90% of lines; the rest are flagged for review.
FR-WEB-01A guest completes a direct booking online and receives confirmation; folio opens automatically.
BR-02No process requires re-typing a transaction already entered in another module.

UAT runs against the live demo (edu-escondida.odoo.com) during a structured cycle; defects triaged by severity; go-live gated on zero criticals.
