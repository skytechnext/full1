Section 08 · Strategic Analysis

08PESTLE Analysis

Macro forces shaping a Philippine dive resort. Environmental and economic factors carry the highest impact; technology is the clearest controllable lever.

🏛 Political Med impact＋

DOT tourism-recovery push and visa liberalisation support inbound dive travel. Local-government (Mabini) permits, marine-protected-area (MPA) rules and boat licensing apply. Geopolitical/travel-advisory swings affect Western source markets. So-what: diversify source markets; keep compliance records in Odoo Documents.

💱 Economic High impact＋

PHP/USD swings change effective rates for foreign guests; PH inflation lifts F&B/fuel/labour costs. PH hotel ADR rose to ≈₱5,987 and RevPAR to ≈₱3,942 in 2025 (≈65% occupancy in Metro Manila). So-what: multi-currency pricing + dynamic yield management in Odoo protect margin.

👥 Social Med impact＋

Rising domestic middle-class diving demand; experiential/“bucket-list” travel; underwater-photography & social-media culture drives discovery. Sustainability-conscious guests favour reef-responsible operators. So-what: lean into photographer niche + sustainability story.

💻 Technological High (controllable)＋

Cloud ERP, channel managers, AI concierge/pricing, mobile payments and online booking are now table-stakes for competitive resorts. So-what: this is the lever Casa Escondida controls — the core of this engagement.

⚖ Legal Med impact＋

BIR tax compliance & 12% VAT, official-receipt rules, dive-liability waivers/medical statements (PADI), maritime & insurance requirements, data-privacy (PH DPA) for guest data. So-what: Odoo Accounting (l10n_ph) + Sign for waivers + access controls.

🌏 Environmental High impact＋

Coral health, typhoon season, climate-driven coral bleaching and seasonal monsoon patterns directly drive diveability and demand. MPA conservation is core to the product. So-what: seasonality must be modelled into pricing & staffing; sustainability is brand-critical.

10Porter's Five Forces

Competitive rivalry and buyer power (via OTAs) are the strongest forces; the macro-diving niche and PADI credentials provide partial insulation.

Force intensity (0–5)

Higher = more pressure on profitability
Competitive rivalry 4.3＋

Dozens of Anilao dive resorts (Buceo, Crystal Blue, Acacia, Aiyanar, Eagle Point…) compete on price, dive quality and photography reputation.

Buyer power (OTAs & guests) 3.8＋

OTAs hold distribution & pricing leverage; well-informed divers compare easily. Direct booking + loyalty reduce this.

Threat of new entrants 3.0＋

Moderate capital + PADI credentialing + prime shorefront create barriers, but new boutique resorts keep appearing.

Supplier power 2.4＋

Gear suppliers, boat crew, food suppliers — generally available; some specialised dive-gear concentration.

Threat of substitutes 2.2＋

Other dive destinations (Cebu, Dauin, Lembeh) & non-diving holidays; mitigated by Anilao's unique macro reputation.

14Customer & Persona Analysis

Five core personas drive demand. The ERP should let Casa Escondida recognise, serve and re-market to each one distinctly.

🐚 Mark · Macro Photographer

Int'l, 35–55, high spend. Wants camera rinse/room, expert guides, repeat critter sites. CLV: high (multi-week, repeat). Books direct or via dive-travel agent.

🏙 Joy · Domestic Weekend Diver

Metro Manila, 28–40. Short stays, value-driven, books via OTA/social. CLV: med, high frequency. Price-sensitive, promo-responsive.

🎓 Leo · Course Student

New diver, OW/AOW. Needs scheduling, waivers, certification. Converts to repeat diver. CLV: growing.

👥 Dive Club Organiser

Group of 8–20. Needs group rates, boat capacity, logistics. CLV: very high per booking. Relationship-driven.

🌅 Non-diving Partner

Travels with diver. Wants F&B, relaxation, day trips. Ancillary-spend opportunity. CLV: med.

💡 ERP implication

Tag every contact by persona in CRM → targeted pricing, packages and automated re-marketing. Track dive history & CLV natively.
