01Engagement summary

A phased, low-risk implementation of one Odoo 19 platform across the whole property — POS & Inventory first (stop the leakage), then PMS + Website + Accounting, then Dive ops, CRM, HR and the AI automation layer — delivered with data migration, training and hypercare. Built and de-risked against a working live demo before go-live.

What you get

  • Configured Odoo across 8+ areas
  • BIR-aligned finance + accounting overhaul
  • Direct-booking website + AI concierge
  • Migrated data & trained team

How it's delivered

  • 3 phases + hypercare
  • Live-demo-first, UAT-gated
  • Standard config over custom code
  • Knowledge transfer throughout

Why it pays back

  • Captured revenue, less leakage
  • Fewer repeat errors
  • Direct bookings at full margin
  • Faster close, live visibility

06Assumptions & exclusions

Assumptions

  • Reliable internet/Starlink at the property.
  • Client team available for discovery, UAT & training.
  • Accountant available for BIR/CAS specifics.
  • POS hardware & printers available or separately procured.
  • Requirements baseline (this set) signed off.

Exclusions

  • Odoo licence fees (billed by Odoo).
  • Hardware, networking, Starlink.
  • BIR filing itself (Odoo prepares; accountant files).
  • Third-party OTA/channel subscriptions (pass-through).
  • Custom code beyond agreed scope (change control).
