Cost & ROI

How much will this cost us?＋

Two parts: Odoo licensing (per active internal user / month — your front desk, dive, kitchen, accounts users) and Technext implementation services (one-time, phased). We size it to your team and scope it by phase so you're never paying for what you're not yet using. We'll give a fixed-scope quote per phase in the SOW.

What's the return — when do we break even? ROI＋

The fast paybacks are OTA-commission savings (15–18% on channel bookings shifted to direct) and +12–22% RevPAR from dynamic pricing — plus the leakage you stop from lost paper slips. On those alone the platform typically pays for itself in roughly 8 months; ancillary revenue and labour savings compound after that.

Are there hidden costs?＋

We list them up front: Odoo user licences, any paid third-party apps (e.g. a channel manager / QloApps subscription if you want OTA auto-sync), payment-gateway fees, and optional hypercare/support. Hosting is included with Odoo Online. No surprises in the SOW.

What if we pay for it and it doesn't work for us?＋

That's why we go phased — Phase 1 (POS + Inventory) is small, fast and proves value before you commit further. You already have a fully-working demo (edu-escondida.odoo.com) populated with your real inventory, so you're deciding on something you can click through, not a promise.

Can we start small?＋

Yes — Phase 1 is just POS + Inventory + basic accounting (your #1 priority: stop cash leakage). We only add PMS, website, dive ops, CRM and AI once you've seen Phase 1 working.

Why Odoo / the platform

Why Odoo and not [other PMS / software]?＋

Most resorts run 4–6 disconnected tools (PMS, POS, accounting, CRM…). Odoo does all of it in one system, so your rooms, restaurant, dive shop and books finally talk to each other — no re-keying, one source of truth. It also has a native Hotel industry app, strong POS, and Philippine localisation.

Are we locked in? Can we get our data out? tech＋

Your data is yours and fully exportable (Excel/CSV/API) any time. Odoo is open-standard; there's no hostage-taking. We document the configuration so another partner could pick it up if ever needed.

Cloud or our own server?＋

We recommend Odoo Online (cloud) — managed hosting, backups and updates included, nothing to maintain on-site. Self-hosting is possible if you ever want full control, but it adds IT overhead you don't need.

What if Odoo raises prices or changes?＋

Pricing is per-user and predictable; you can add/remove users as your team changes. Because your data is exportable and the config is documented, you're never trapped — that's your leverage.

Bookings, Booking.com & the website

Does it handle our room bookings and the website?＋

Yes — the Hotel app's Schedule shows every room and reservation, and your website now takes online bookings (guests pick dates, room, breakfast & guests, and book). It's already live in the demo.

Will it sync with Booking.com so Eloisa stops aligning rates by hand?＋

Yes, via a channel manager (e.g. QloApps connector). It two-way-syncs rates, availability and reservations so Booking.com updates automatically — ending the manual alignment and protecting against overbooking. Note this needs a paid channel-manager subscription, which we'll quote separately.

Can it stop double-bookings?＋

Yes — once availability is centralised, the system blocks a room that's taken and an overbooking-guard process flags any channel conflict. No more relying on memory or a spreadsheet.

Will the site work in Chinese for our overseas guests?＋

Yes — the website (and the AI chatbot) support Simplified + Traditional Chinese, with your proper Chinese brand name. That directly serves the HK/Taiwan/China markets Andrew wants to grow.

Tax, VAT & BIR

Is the POS BIR-accredited? Will our receipts be compliant? important＋

The new POS must be BIR-registered to issue compliant official receipts — your current one isn't (the old accredited one lapsed). We flag this as a must-do before real selling and help with the accreditation path. Odoo supports the OR/invoice format and 12% VAT.

Does it handle 12% VAT and PH reporting?＋

Yes — Philippine localisation (l10n_ph) is installed, 12% VAT is applied on sales/POS/invoices, and VAT & financial reports are built in. (Switching the full chart of accounts to the PH template needs a clean-books window, which we'll plan.)

Security & access

Is our data safe in the cloud? tech＋

Odoo Online runs on enterprise-grade hosting with encryption, daily backups and redundancy — far safer than a spreadsheet on one PC or a server in the office. You also get point-in-time restore.

Can we control who sees what? Staff shouldn't see the books.＋

Yes — role-based access. Front desk, dive, kitchen and accounts each see only their apps; financials and approvals are restricted to you and the accountant. POS uses shared department logins as you requested.

What about passwords and that API key we shared?＋

Please rotate the account password and API key that were shared during this build — treat anything sent in chat as exposed. We store nothing sensitive in the project repo, and we'll set up proper per-user logins and 2-factor for admins.

What if the internet goes down — does the POS stop?＋

Odoo POS keeps working offline and syncs when the connection returns — so the restaurant and dive shop can keep selling even if the line drops. We also recommend a backup connection/UPS for the front office.

Staff & change management

Our staff aren't techy — will they actually use it?＋

The POS and daily screens are tap-simple (easier than the paper-and-chat juggling today). We provide role-based training, quick-reference cards, champions and a hypercare period. Adoption — not technology — is the real risk, so we manage it deliberately (ADKAR).

What if staff resist the change?＋

We start with the wins they feel (less paperwork, no lost orders, faster billing), involve champions early, and roll out in phases. Each role has a short guide. Resistance usually fades once the first payday/close is painless.

How many devices do we need?＋

Start with 3 shared POS units (dive shop priority, then dining/front desk), non-waterproof is fine. Scale to ~5 as you settle in.

Operations specifics

Does the kitchen get the orders automatically?＋

Yes — a Kitchen Display shows tickets the moment they're punched at POS, with prep stages. No more paper chits walked to the kitchen or lost.

Can charges go to the room and be one bill at checkout?＋

Yes — “charge to room” posts dives, bar and restaurant onto one guest folio; checkout produces a single consolidated VAT receipt (group or individual, as the guest prefers).

What about dive waivers, gear and certifications?＋

Digital waivers/medical (e-sign), serial-tracked gear with servicing reminders, and instructor certifications tracked with renewal alerts — closing the compliance gaps that are on paper today.

Multiple currencies for foreign guests?＋

Supported — you quote/accept in PHP (as you do now) and can display/settle other currencies for overseas guests where needed.

The bread-and-butter automations are concrete and already configured (review requests, low-stock reorder, overbooking guard, dynamic pricing, dive daily briefing). The AI concierge is an assist with human approval. Nothing depends on hype to deliver the ROI.

Cash leakage. Every sale — room, dive, bar, retail — is captured and tied to live stock and the books. If it's punched in, it's billed and counted. That's the foundation everything else builds on.
